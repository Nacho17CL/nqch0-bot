Privacy Policy

Nqch0 Bot — Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Nqch0 Bot handles information when users interact with the bot and its supported integrations.

2. Information We Access

Nqch0 Bot may access publicly available information from supported platforms in order to provide automated notifications.

For TikTok, the application may access basic profile information and publicly available videos associated with the authorized TikTok account.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through supported APIs is used only to provide the bot's notification functionality, such as detecting newly published content and notifying members of the associated Discord community.

4. Data Storage

Nqch0 Bot does not intentionally store personal information beyond what is necessary to operate its notification features.

Authentication credentials and access tokens, when required, are stored securely and are not publicly disclosed.

5. Third-Party Services

Nqch0 Bot may interact with third-party platforms including TikTok, Twitch, YouTube, Kick and X/Twitter.

These platforms have their own privacy policies and terms of service. Nqch0 Bot is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

6. Data Sharing

Nqch0 Bot does not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with the application's integrations by contacting the project owner.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the project owner through the official Nqch0 community channels.
